Self Introduction

Hi! I'm Kavya S, and I'm currently pursuing my final year B.Tech in Information Technology at VSB Engineering College. I'm from a technical background and over the last few years in college, I developed an interest in programming and software development.

Initially, coding was difficult for me, but by practicing regularly and building small projects, I became more comfortable with it. Among the technologies I've learned, I enjoy working with Java the most because I like solving logical problems and understanding how programs work behind the scenes.

Whenever I build a project, I try to understand the complete flow instead of just writing code. If I face an issue, I usually debug it myself or read documentation and learn from it. That habit has helped me improve my problem-solving skills.

One quality that describes me well is that I'm a quick learner. If I'm given a new tool or technology, I may not know everything immediately, but I'm willing to learn and adapt as required. I also enjoy working in a team — discussing ideas and finding solutions together improved both my communication and collaboration skills.

At this stage of my career, my goal is simple: I want to join a company where I can learn from experienced professionals, improve my technical skills, and contribute to real-world software projects.
